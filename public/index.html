Paul Robeson

Ol' Man River

Warsaw, Poland, 1949

Archival footage compiled in 'Paul Robeson: Tribute to an Artist' (1979), a documentary short directed by Saul J. Turell and narrated by Sidney Poitier. The performance clip is allegedly from Robeson's European concert tour in Warsaw, 1949. The documentary traces his evolving performances of 'Ol' Man River' — from Oscar Hammerstein's original text to Robeson's own protest rewrite. Won the Academy Award for Documentary Short Subject at the 52nd Academy Awards (1980). en.wikipedia.org imdb.com archive.org criterion.com

            another machine's steganography library. Each image encodes audio into its pixel values using a traversal
            pattern, keymap, and combine operation. No audio files, no server,
